### Content and Schemas (Markdown)

Instructions for creating content and descriptions of the frontmatter structures for each collection.

#### 1. General Rules

- All content files must be located in `src/content/{collection}/`.
- File names must use the `kebab-case.md` format.
- Images must be stored in `public/images/` or next to the `.md` files.
